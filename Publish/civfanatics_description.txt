[SIZE=6][B]Anthroprocenia[/B][/SIZE]

Anthroprocenia records your Civilization VII game and adds an interactive historical [B]Map View[/B]: a replay timeline, geographic labels, and configurable map layers.

[SIZE=5][B]Features[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]History replay[/B]: scrub, step, or play back every recorded turn, with territory, settlements, roads, and units.
[*][B]Chronicles and families[/B]: expandable dated histories and modeled family trees. Date displays include the calendar date and turn number.
[*][B]Geography[/B]: real-world names and subdivisions on supported Earth maps, plus generated geography on other maps. Filter regions, islands, water bodies, rivers, mountains and continents.
[*][B]Townscapes[/B]: zoom in to see detailed settlements with streets, buildings, and a large central landmark for each district. Roofs appear progressively while road preparation continues.
[*][B]Population[/B]: walking residents with names, genders, occupations, personality traits, and homes. The Population panel groups people by civilization, settlement, and home location, sorts by occupation, shows occupation counts on hover, and can pause the walkers.
[*][B]Layers[/B]: toggle borders, labels, rivers, natural features, continent boundaries, fog of war, and more.
[*][B]Controls[/B]: mouse, touch, keyboard, and controller support.
[*][B]Calendars and pacing[/B]: Realistic and Realistic x2-x6 speeds, with annual, seasonal and monthly calendars. Skip Ahead uses native single-player autoplay with pause and Stop controls.
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]Installation[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*]Download and open the zip.
[*]Extract the [I]anthroprocenia[/I] folder into:
[CODE]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Firaxis Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VII\Mods\[/CODE]
(Create the [I]Mods[/I] folder if it does not exist.) The result should be [I]Mods\anthroprocenia\anthroprocenia.modinfo[/I].
[*]Remove any older Anthroprocenia copies, including a Steam Workshop subscription, so only one is loaded.
[*]Restart the game and enable [B]Anthroprocenia[/B] under [B]Add-Ons[/B].
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]Usage[/B][/SIZE]
Open [B]Map View[/B] from the in-game HUD. Drag or use the arrow keys to pan, and use the mouse wheel or +/- to zoom. Space plays or pauses the replay. Open the Population panel with the button at the top center of the map.

Includes gameplay setup, speed and calendar changes and is marked as affecting saved games. Back up saves before updating, choose your speed when starting a new game, and fully restart after installation. YnAMP is optional and supplies Giant Earth-specific options.

Native enemy-hover combat estimates and the red movement-style attack path remain unfinished.
