Version 5
- Map View: clicking a walking person no longer opens the city view; hovering a townscape building no longer outlines it.
- Residents show what they are doing when hovered (based on occupation, gender, and era), e.g. "Tending to Grounds", "Buying Supplies", "Traveling to {place}", "Heading Home".
- Residents travel further and more often, stopping at trees, riverbanks, fields, and resources.
- Population is spread more unevenly: most people live in city centers and urban districts, few on rural improvements.

Version 4
- Map View: city dots and decorative unit dots are hidden while townscapes are shown.
- Townscapes: ten times as many, smaller buildings in the same layouts, plus one large central building per district.
- Population panel: moved to the top center with an icon; grouped by Home Location; sort by occupation; gender column; occupation-count tooltips on every level; pause/play button for walking residents.
- Hovering a panel entry points a blinking arrow at that person, home, or tile (at the screen edge when it is off screen). Hovering a person, home, or tile on the map highlights the matching panel rows.
- Residents avoid trees, rivers, and buildings where possible, entering buildings through doors.
- Compact, ID-based storage for names, personality traits, and homes.
